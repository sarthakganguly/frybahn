Privacy Center

No Registration & Personal Data Philosophy

At Frybahn, we believe in instant, friction-free gaming. We do not require user accounts, email registration, logins, or the input of personal contact information to play any games on our portal. We do not natively collect, request, or store your name, email address, physical location, or billing details.

Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Disclosures

We monetize Frybahn using advertisement links and banners served by Google AdSense and other third-party networks. To provide AdSense compliance, please review the following disclosures regarding how advertisements are served on this website:

  • DART Cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on Frybahn. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted advertisements to our users based on their visits to this site and other websites on the Internet.
  • Personalized Advertising: Third-party advertising vendors and networks may serve advertisements on this website. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies to measure advertisement effectiveness and display ads tailored to your browsing interests.

Opt-Out Rights & Cookie Disabling Options

You have full control over cookie tracking. You can choose to decline personalized advertising or block cookies entirely using the options below:

  • Google Ads Settings: You can opt out of Google's personalized advertising entirely by visiting the Google Ads Settings page.
  • Third-Party Opt-Out: You can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the AboutAds Info Portal or the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) Opt-Out Page.
  • Browser Configuration: You can configure your browser (such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, or Microsoft Edge) to refuse all cookies, or to notify you when a cookie is set. Consult your browser's "Help" documentation to adjust these configurations.

Local Storage Utilisation

Many of the single-page browser games featured on Frybahn utilize the browser's localStorage or sessionStorage API to save gameplay states, settings, and local high scores. This data is stored strictly on your local device and is never transmitted, processed, or saved on Frybahn's servers.

Log Files & Traffic Analytics

Like most static file web servers, our host system automatically maintains standard access log files. These files log non-identifiable technical data, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized in log structures)
  • Browser User-Agent strings and operating system types
  • Referring pages, exit pages, and date/time timestamps

We use this data solely to monitor server health, mitigate malicious automated traffic, debug platform errors, and evaluate general category usage trends.

European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

For players residing within the European Economic Area (EEA), we process all data in compliance with GDPR. Under GDPR, you hold the following rights regarding any data third parties or trackers collect:

  • Right to Object: You can object to the processing of your browsing patterns for personalized advertising.
  • Consent Management: You can revoke advertising consents at any time using the opt-out links listed in this policy or via our cookie consent settings.
  • Information Queries: Because we hold no login or profile data, we cannot access, delete, or package individual records unless you supply a specific network identifier.

California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

For California consumers, the CCPA grants you specific choices regarding your personal data. We do not sell your personal details. However, third-party advertising partners may collect device identifiers and browsing data to serve relevant ads. Under CCPA, you hold the following rights:

  • Right to Know: The categories of personal data collected about you (primarily device IDs and cookies for ad-tech).
  • Right to Opt-Out: The right to direct third-party networks not to "sell" or "share" your personal information for targeted advertisements. You can exercise this opt-out option by adjusting your cookie selections or visiting the AboutAds CCPA portal.
  • Right to Deletion & Non-Discrimination: Request deletion of collected browsing logs and receive equal access to all games without penalty.

Third-Party Games & External Links

This privacy center policy applies exclusively to Frybahn. The games listed on our platform are loaded inside sandboxed <iframe> frames to prevent unauthorized cross-origin access. However, some games may contain external links to developers' home pages or code repositories. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or cookie tracking implemented on external domains.

Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, your CCPA/GDPR consumer choices, or the cookies used on our gaming portal, please contact us via email at: privacy@frybahn.com.
