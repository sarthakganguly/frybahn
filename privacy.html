Privacy Center

No Registration, No Tracking

Frybahn does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information. We do not use persistent tracking cookies or collect data on your gaming habits.

Local Storage

Some games may use your browser's local storage to save high scores or game progress. This data remains on your device and is never sent to our servers.

External Links

Games may contain links to the developers' websites or source code. Please be aware that once you leave Frybahn, you are subject to the privacy policies of those external sites.
