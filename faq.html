Frequently Asked Questions

Is Freibahn really free?

Yes! All games on Freibahn are completely free to play. We don't require any subscriptions or one-time payments.

Do I need to create an account?

No. We believe in instant access. You can play any game in our catalogue without ever having to log in or register.

Can I play on mobile?

Absolutely. Most of our games are built with responsive web technologies and support touch controls for gaming on the go.

How can I add my game to Freibahn?

Check out our Developers page for information on how to submit your title to our catalogue.
