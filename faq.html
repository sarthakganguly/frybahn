Frequently Asked Questions

Is Frybahn really free?

Yes! All games on Frybahn are completely free to play. We don't require any subscriptions or one-time payments.

Do I need to create an account?

No. We believe in instant access. You can play any game in our catalogue without ever having to log in or register.

Can I play on mobile?

Absolutely. Some of the games are built with responsive web technologies and support touch controls for gaming on the go.

How can I add my game to Frybahn?

Check out our Developers page for step-by-step guidelines on packaging your game, catalog syntax, and opening a Pull Request.

Do the games save my progress or high scores?

Yes, many games on our platform support progress saving. Since we do not require user accounts, games use your web browser's local storage (such as localStorage or IndexedDB) to save high scores, game states, and progress. Please note that clearing your browser cache or history may erase this saved data.

Why is a game showing a blank screen or failing to load?

Most game loading issues can be solved by checking the following:

  • Ad-blockers & Privacy Extensions: Some aggressive ad-blockers block scripts running inside sandboxed frames. Try whitelisting Frybahn or disabling your extension temporarily.
  • WebGL Support: Modern 3D or hardware-intensive HTML5 games require WebGL. Ensure that hardware acceleration is enabled in your browser settings (e.g. chrome://settings/system in Google Chrome).
  • Corrupted Cache: Try a hard refresh (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R) to clear any outdated resources.

Can I play Frybahn games offline?

Frybahn is designed as an online web gaming hub, and accessing the portal requires an active internet connection to download and stream game assets. However, once a game is fully loaded in your browser session, some simple HTML5 games may continue to function offline until the tab is closed or reloaded.

What are the keyboard controls for navigating the portal?

Frybahn features a complete console-style keyboard navigation system for a seamless game launcher experience:

  • Use Arrow Keys (Up, Down, Left, Right) to move focus across categories and game cards in the catalog grid.
  • Press Enter to open a game's details modal or launch the gameplay overlay.
  • Press Escape (Esc) to close details modals or return to the main dashboard.
  • Press the Forward Slash (/) key to jump directly into the search bar.

Are these games safe for my device?

Absolutely. Your security is our priority. Every game on Frybahn runs within a highly restricted, sandboxed <iframe> element with strict origin controls. This completely isolates the games from the main website and your system, preventing unauthorized script executions, cookie access, or redirect attempts.
