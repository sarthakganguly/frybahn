Frequently Asked Questions

Is Frybahn really free?

Yes! All games on Frybahn are completely free to play. We don't require any subscriptions or one-time payments.

Do I need to create an account?

No. We believe in instant access. You can play any game in our catalogue without ever having to log in or register.

Can I play on mobile?

Absolutely. Some of the games are built with responsive web technologies and support touch controls for gaming on the go.

How can I add my game to Frybahn?

Check out our Developers page for step-by-step guidelines on packaging your game, catalog syntax, and opening a Pull Request.
