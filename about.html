About Freibahn

Freibahn was born from a simple idea: gaming should be instant, free, and accessible to everyone with a web browser. Our mission is to bridge the gap between high-quality indie titles and players looking for immediate entertainment.

Inspired by the sleek, immersive interfaces of modern gaming consoles, we built Freibahn to be more than just a list of links. It's a curated experience designed to showcase the incredible talent of the browser gaming community.
